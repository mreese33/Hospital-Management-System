For this assignment, I created two different systems using a binary tree and a min-heap. I think that using a tree for the doctors makes
the most sense because it allows the hospital to have a clear structure of who reports to who. Having everything organized this way 
makes it easier to understand the different levels of management and who is responsible for certain employees. Instead of having all 
the doctors in one long list, the tree allows everything to be connected based on their reporting structure.

The three different traversal methods can be useful depending on what information the hospital is trying to look at. Preorder would 
be helpful when looking at the structure starting with the head doctor and then moving down to the other doctors. Inorder allows the
system to go through the doctors starting from the left side of the tree, then the manager, and finally the right side. Postorder
could be helpful when the hospital wants to review information from the doctors before getting to their manager. I think having
these different methods is important because not every situation requires the information to be looked at in the same order.

For the emergency priority queue, I used a min-heap because patients should be treated based on how serious their condition is 
rather than when they arrived. The heap makes sure that the patient with the highest priority is always at the top. As new patients 
arrive or others are treated, the system reorganizes itself to keep the correct order. I think this is especially important in an 
emergency room because situations can change quickly, and the hospital needs a way to keep track of which patients need help first.
